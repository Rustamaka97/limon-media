# La Limonariya — Privacy Policy / Maxfiylik siyosati

_Last updated: 2026-08-27_

**LaLimon Server** is an internal tool used exclusively by La Limonariya restaurant
(Navoiy, Uzbekistan) to manage its own Instagram business account
(@la_limonariya): publishing content, reading engagement metrics, replying to
comments and messages, and managing advertising for its own posts.

## What data we access
- Content, comments, and metrics of **our own** Instagram business account only.
- No data of third-party users is collected, stored, or shared.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not collect personal data of visitors or customers through this app.
- We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
- We do not track users across websites or apps.

## Data storage
Operational data (post metrics, publishing queue) is stored on the restaurant's
own private server and is not shared externally.

## Data deletion
This app serves a single business account owned by us. To request deletion of
any data related to interactions with our account, contact us:

- Instagram: [@la_limonariya](https://instagram.com/la_limonariya)
- Phone: +998 95 832 99 99
